# Activity 1 — RCC Beam Design

**Chosen member:** Simply supported singly reinforced rectangular RCC beam (provisional; faculty approval required).

**Problem:** Design a 5.0 m simply supported beam, 250 mm wide and 450 mm overall deep, for self-weight, 8 kN/m superimposed dead load, 1.2 kN/m finishes and 3 kN/m live load. Adopt M25 concrete, Fe500 steel, 25 mm cover and 16 mm main bars. Use IS 456:2000 and IS 875 Parts 1 and 2.

The verified calculation path is: beam self-weight = bD×25; factored UDL = 1.5(DL+LL); Mu = wuL²/8; Vu = wuL/2. Effective depth is D−cover−bar radius. Required tensile steel follows the singly reinforced IS 456 flexural expression; it is checked against minimum steel, 4% maximum steel and the limiting moment 0.138fckbd² for Fe500. A basic span/depth screen, development length and reinforcement sketch are included. Shear/stirrup design, modification factors and final detailing must be completed/checked manually for the submitted design.
